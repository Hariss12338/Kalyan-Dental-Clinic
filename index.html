About Kalyan Dental Clinic

Kalyan Dental Clinic was established in 2004. Led by Dr. Sabina, a reputed dentist in Kalyan with over 22 years of experience, the clinic provides advanced and holistic dental care using the latest technologies and modern dental techniques.

We believe in patient-centric care with a focus on comfort, precision, and lasting results. Our team is committed to transforming smiles and improving oral health with cutting-edge treatments and compassionate service.
